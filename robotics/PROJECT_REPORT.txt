1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ABSTRACT
RoboNav-SLAM is an advanced autonomous mobile robotics platform capable of 
real-time Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM), autonomous indoor 
path planning, and obstacle avoidance in GPS-denied environments.

Core Technologies:
- 360-degree 2D RPLIDAR Laser Scanning (12m range, 10Hz)
- RGB-D / Wide-Angle Camera for Visual Odometry & AprilTag Relocalization
- ESP32 240MHz Microcontroller with Dual Quadrature Encoder PID Control (50Hz)
- Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) Multi-Sensor Fusion
- Log-Odds Bayesian Occupancy Grid SLAM with Scan-Matching
- Global Planner: A* Search with Obstacle Costmap Inflation
- Local Planner: Dynamic Window Approach (DWA) Collision Evasion (20Hz)
- Autonomous Frontier Exploration Engine for 100% Unsupervised Mapping

2. MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLES
- Differential Drive Kinematics:
  v = r * (omega_R + omega_L) / 2
  omega = r * (omega_R - omega_L) / L
- Log-Odds Occupancy Grid SLAM Update:
  l_t(m_i) = l_{t-1}(m_i) + inv_sensor_model(m_i, x_t, z_t) - l_0
  where inv_sensor_model = +0.85 (hit) and -0.35 (free ray)
- DWA Objective Function:
  G(v, w) = alpha * heading(v, w) + beta * dist(v, w) + gamma * velocity(v, w)
