1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
The AIoT Landslide & Flash-Flood Early Warning System is an autonomous environmental
monitoring and disaster prediction platform designed for the flood/landslide prone
high ranges of Kerala (Wayanad, Idukki, Nilambur).
Key innovations:
- Automated Siphoning Rain Gauge using Ultrasonic Acoustic Tracking + 12V Solenoid Flush
- Real-time Stream & River Level Velocity Tracking
- Volumetric Soil Moisture Saturation Sensor
- Microclimate Atmospheric Monitoring via DHT22
- Embedded TinyML Sensor Fusion Classifier (Multivariate Logistic Inference)
- Local High-Decibel Acoustic Siren + Blynk IoT 2.0 Cloud Dashboard

2. SCIENTIFIC WORKING PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------
A. Soil Mechanics & Pore-Water Pressure:
   Terzaghi's effective stress: sigma' = sigma - u
   Shear strength tau_f decreases as moisture saturation approaches 100%.

B. Ultrasonic Solenoid Rain Gauge:
   Effective Rain (mm) = Chamber Height * (Chamber Area / Funnel Area)
   Instant Rain Rate (mm/hr) = (Delta H / Delta T) * Area Ratio * 3600
   When water reaches 135mm, 12V Solenoid opens for 3 seconds to flush, logs timestamp,
   and increments cumulative rainfall count.

C. TinyML Hazard Classification:
   z = w0 + w1*(Moisture%) + w2*(RainRate) + w3*(CumRain) + w4*(StreamRise)
   Probability P(Hazard) = 1 / (1 + exp(-z)) * 100%

3. HARDWARE PIN ASSIGNMENTS (ESP32)
-----------------------------------
- Soil Moisture Analog:   GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6)
- Stream Ultrasonic TRIG: GPIO 5
- Stream Ultrasonic ECHO: GPIO 18
- Rain Gauge Ultrasonic TRIG: GPIO 19
- Rain Gauge Ultrasonic ECHO: GPIO 21
- 12V Solenoid Relay:     GPIO 22 (Active HIGH)
- DHT22 Temp/Humidity:    GPIO 4 (4.7k Pull-up)
- Active Alert Siren:     GPIO 23
- Safe LED (Green):       GPIO 2
- Alert LED (Red):        GPIO 15

4. BLYNK IOT 2.0 VIRTUAL PINS
-----------------------------
V0: Hazard Index (%)
V1: Soil Moisture (%)
V2: Stream Water Level (cm)
V3: Rain Intensity (mm/hr)
V4: Cumulative Rainfall (mm)
V5: Temperature (C)
V6: Humidity (%)
V7: Solenoid Flush Count
V8: Risk Level Text Banner
