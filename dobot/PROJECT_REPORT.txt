1. PROJECT SUMMARY
------------------
The Dobot Magician Lite is a high-precision 4-Axis articulated educational and industrial
robot arm offering +-0.2mm repeatability, 250g payload capacity, and 340mm reach radius.
Paired with the Magic Box MicroPython controller, the robot performs autonomous visual
inspection, color classification, and coordinate-mapped pick-and-place manipulation.

2. SCIENTIFIC & MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLES
---------------------------------------
A. Inverse Kinematics:
   Converts target Cartesian position (X, Y, Z, R) into motor joint angles (Theta 1 to 4)
   using Law of Cosines and geometric arm link decomposition.

B. Perspective Homography Matrix (Vision Calibration):
   Transforms camera pixel coordinates (u, v) into robot base frame coordinates (X, Y):
   [X_robot, Y_robot, 1]^T = H * [u_pixel, v_pixel, 1]^T

C. PTP Motion Modes:
   - JUMP Mode: Trajectory with automatic vertical ascent, horizontal transit, and descent
   - MOVL Mode: Straight-line Cartesian linear path
   - MOVJ Mode: Coordinated multi-joint point-to-point motion

3. HARDWARE & PORTS

2. Precision Calligraphy & PCB Tracing
3. Real-Time Teleoperation & Camera Gesture Control
